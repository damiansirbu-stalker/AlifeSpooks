# AlifeSpooks source licensing

The license of every sound source AlifeSpooks ships from. The mod ships only free content or content whose author granted permission. The readme Licensing section is derived from this record.

## Principle

GSC owns the original S.T.A.L.K.E.R. game audio, and the scene uses it under GSC's long tolerance of modding. A pack that re-encodes or remasters GSC material does not own it. Any sound that exists in vanilla Anomaly or in the standalone builds is GSC-original, sourced from there, and free of a pack author's claim. A pack author's terms govern only the sound they created from scratch.

The moddb License field is a coarse default. moddb shows "Proprietary" for anything without a named open license, including licenses that permit reuse, so the field alone does not settle terms. The record below reads each author's own words.

## The request message

Generic, sent to the pending authors:

Hi, I'm Damian. I work on the X-Ray engine and make mods for Anomaly and GAMMA (https://www.moddb.com/members/damian-sirbu/addons). Right now I'm building AlifeSpooks, a horror ambience mod. It drops the old sound-channel setup and runs a director that places dread sounds around the player, based on where they are and what's near, so the zone stays tense and alive instead of looping the same bed. I'd love to include some of your sounds in it, fully credited to you, in the free release. Would that be alright? And if there's anything you'd rather I not use, just say so and I'll leave it out.
